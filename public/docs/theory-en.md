## 6. Chronological Timeline of the Photon Concept (1704 - Present)

### 1704 — Sir Isaac Newton: Light as High-Speed Material Corpuscles
- **Era:** Classical | **Theory:** Classical Mechanics
- **Paradigm Shift:** Early ballistic model: light as miniature cannonballs emitted from luminous sources.
- **Formula:** $\vec{p} = m \vec{v}$ (Light consists of localized corpuscles carrying mass and momentum travelling along straight paths in vacuum.)
- **Key Experiment:** Dispersion of light through prisms (Opticks 1704). Newton explained refraction by attractive forces between particles and the medium.
- **Full Explanation:** Newton proposed that light is composed of minute particles (corpuscles). These corpuscles obeyed mechanical laws, travelling straight through empty space and reflecting like elastic spheres off boundaries. While intuitive, this ballistic model was entirely helpless in explaining diffraction and interference fringes.
- *Quote:* "Are not the rays of light very small bodies emitted from shining substances?"

### 1801 — Thomas Young: Light Interferes Like Water Ripples
- **Era:** Classical | **Theory:** Wave Optics
- **Paradigm Shift:** Solid particles cannot cancel each other out to create darkness! Light undeniably possesses wave characteristics.
- **Formula:** $\Delta d = d \sin \theta = m \lambda$ (Path difference is an integer multiple of wavelength, generating constructive interference (bright bands) and destructive minima (dark bands).)
- **Key Experiment:** Shining light through two narrow parallel slits produces an alternating pattern of bright and dark fringes on a distant screen.
- **Full Explanation:** Young's double-slit experiment dealt a decisive blow to Newtonian corpuscular theory. If light were a hail of bullets, two slits would produce only two bright stripes. Instead, a sprawling interference pattern appeared. Two wave peaks add constructively to produce brightness; a peak meeting a trough cancels completely into total darkness.
- *Quote:* "Light added to light can produce darkness."

### 1865 — James Clerk Maxwell: Light is an Electromagnetic Field Oscillation
- **Era:** Classical | **Theory:** Classical Electrodynamics
- **Paradigm Shift:** The concept of "FIELD" is born. Empty space is not a void, but permeated by continuous electromagnetic fields.

- **Key Experiment:** Computing electromagnetic wave speed from electric and magnetic constants (c = 1/√(ε₀μ₀)), matching optical measurements of light speed.
